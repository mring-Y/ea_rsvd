# Manuscript asset correspondence

Tables 1 and Algorithm 1 define diagnostic regions and offline selection.
Tables 2–4, 6–11 and Figures 1–8 use measured records exported in
`evidence/submission/` and the analysis scripts in the repository scripts
directory. Its source manifest records the exact source/export hashes.
Table 11 uses `budget_cohort.py` and `budget_fixed_cohort.csv`, retaining all
25 paths at every budget cap. Original available-case summaries are retained
only for provenance. The paired-device and complete
solver timings have different output and timing scopes. They are not pooled.

Table 5: `evidence/table5_audit.csv` provides direct complete-factor residuals,
identical-input FP64 references and factor hashes. Its grid mean diagnostic
time is from the original 450-state probe-inclusive grid; Figure 5 retains
those measured energy diagnostics. The audit does not reconstruct a missing
historical factor file. It reruns the selected configuration with exported
complete factors and explicitly validates those factors.

Section 6.2: `evidence/core450.csv`, `matched_replay.py` and
`evidence/policy_comparison/` provide common-target rank-16 reanalysis.
Section 6.3: `evidence/additional72.csv` and `evidence/heldout_validation/`
provide four additional structures and preserve every unattained state.
The matrix list/grid was frozen; the added route comparison is post hoc.

Table 12 and Section 6.6: complete-output campaign code is in published_gpu;
`evidence/primme_expanded_groups.csv` gives all 60 matched expanded groups.
`summarize_primme.py` recomputes five-matrix and leave-one-matrix summaries.
The expanded reconstruction comparison has two PRIMME-faster A800 groups.
All seven grids retain their environment, build and matrix manifests under
`evidence/submission/published_gpu/`; `environment_manifest.json` records
the original/export hashes and the omitted local paths and GPU UUIDs.

Table 13 and Section 6.7: `evidence/cache/` contains all 18 candidate timing
summaries and saved warm samples; `analyse_cached.py` recomputes medians.
Its historical break-even fields compare to independent solves, not an optimal
final-output cache; those repeated-rank counts are no longer manuscript claims.
`reviewer_evidence.py` recomputes the three-rank cost model and rank-reference
checks. `evidence/reviewer_evidence.json` also records the six rank-32 prefix
audits and source hashes; use the script's optional factor/input arguments to
recompute those checks after generating the omitted factors.
`evidence/cached_validation.json` gives direct cached/fresh residual checks.
Full matrices, factors and binaries are omitted
from this small source supplement and can be regenerated by the runners.
The independent 18-state confirmation is in `evidence/independent18_timing.csv`;
it is a distinct backend timing check and is not used for cache amortization.
